# Research Summary — Downstream Devices-to-Semiconductor Output (19 October 2026)

The global semiconductor industry has a strong AI/memory growth narrative, but U.S. computer and communications-equipment output is not necessarily a leading signal for domestic chip manufacturing. In 108 purged expanding-window forecasts using 189 official monthly G.17 observations, the proposed demand-output gap achieved RMSE **4.746 pp**, worse than macro controls (**4.713 pp**) and semiconductor persistence (**4.468 pp**). The moving-block bootstrap 95% interval for the incremental RMSE improvement was **[-0.149, 0.077] pp**, and the exploratory 2,000-replicate block-permutation test did not support the factor (**p≈0.796**). **Verdict:** no robust incremental macro factor and no tested equity alpha; source history is latest-revised, without archival point-in-time vintages, company fundamentals, or equity returns.
