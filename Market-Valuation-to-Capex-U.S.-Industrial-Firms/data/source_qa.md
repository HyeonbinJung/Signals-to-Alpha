# Data integrity and vintage audit — 2026-10-27

- `statsmodels.datasets.grunfeld` version **0.14.6**, accessed from the installed local package on 2026-10-27, provides 220 observed rows, 11 named firms, annual 1935–1954, positive `invest`, `value`, `capital`. Original source: Grunfeld 1958 dissertation, as reconstructed by Kleiber & Zeileis 2010. Package docs explicitly call the data public domain.
- Raw file is frozen without edits and SHA-256 recorded in `raw_sha256.txt`; `analysis.load_raw` checks balanced panel, duplicates, missingness and positive values. The dataset is **not** historical public release-vintage data; 1958 reconstruction of 1935–1954 data cannot have been available at each formation date.
- Critical source-version disagreement: `statsmodels` 0.14.6 has **US Steel 1946 capital = 132.6**, while CRAN AER documentation identifies **232.6** as the corrected value and 132.6 as a Greene (2003) error. The main result intentionally retains the package's frozen raw value; `us_steel_1946_capital_corrected` recomputes an explicitly disclosed sensitivity using the documented correction. This is a major reason to avoid PIT or production claims.
- Other CRAN AER documented corrections: US Steel investment 1940 **361.6** vs erroneous 261.6, and investment 1952 **645.5** vs erroneous 645.2; statsmodels snapshot has 361.6 and 645.5. These are spot checks, not a full independent audit.
- Market `value` is **not** equity total return. It includes common/preferred stock and debt, and for IBM/Chrysler/Westinghouse may average December 31 and following January 31. Signals use t−1 value only for target year t, with conceptual June-t formation; actual historical filing availability cannot be independently established.
- `capital` is 1947-price plant/equipment stock, `invest` gross investment in 1947 dollars, `value` market valuation in 1947 dollars. All outcomes are **actual firm-level capital investment**, not macro production, stock return, excess return, or risk-adjusted trading performance.
- Industry map is 2026 researcher inference from firm names and broad activities; **not** an audited vintage segment-exposure or time-varying revenue-weight map. It is used only for description and chart grouping, not to tune predictive factor weights.
