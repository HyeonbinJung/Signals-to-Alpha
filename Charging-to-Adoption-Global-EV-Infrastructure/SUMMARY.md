### 🔌 Charging-to-Adoption — global EV charging infrastructure
The IEA 2024 historical snapshot supports a plausible long-run charging-infrastructure demand theme but does not establish causal or investable alpha. In 96 expanding-window country-year forecasts (2018–2023), adding lagged charging features marginally improved controls-only RMSE from 4.483 to 4.468 pp, but underperformed a simple momentum baseline (4.416 pp). A 1,000-refit within-year placebo gave exploratory p≈0.132, and a 2,000-draw year-block bootstrap interval included zero. **Verdict: structural EV industry thesis plausible; retrospective non-point-in-time demand predictor unproven, with no company fundamentals or equity returns tested.**
