# Data provenance and independent audit limits

The frozen four-series G.17 CSV is copied byte-for-byte from `Semiconductor-Device-Gap-to-Alpha-U.S.-Chip-Manufacturing/data/fred_g17_snapshot_2011_2026.csv` in the same repository. This run independently checked its exact Git blob SHA `3d111120b93de0a5eb153d794772225f5dd1a6c4`, 189 monthly dates (2011-01 through 2026-09), strictly positive observations, no missing values and monthly continuity. It is **not an independently sourced new G.17 vintage**. This is transparent data reuse with a new industry outcome, research question and predeclared factor. Original series publishers are the Federal Reserve Board and FRED. Official G.17 calendar lists 2026-10-16 for September 2026; historic month-by-month revisions and first-available values are UNKNOWN.

At formation month t, the most recent usable source observation is t-2, a conservative release buffer but **not** proof that 2026-revised historical observations were available then. Training labels must have target observation u+h <= t-2. Predeclared horizon h=3 months. All fits use expanding data and train-only scaling. Multiple prior studies, candidate horizons and lags create selection/multiple-testing risk. No security universe, delisted stocks, transaction costs, portfolio construction or market-return series are present. The descriptive 2025 issuer filing rows are not a backtest and are not used in regression.

The G.17 release history and NAICS classification are cited in the source register. Direct official bulk data extraction and ALFRED historical vintage reconciliation are future work; no data were fabricated to fill this gap. The new research is a **retrospective macro lead-indicator falsification pilot**.
